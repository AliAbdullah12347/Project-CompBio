 !! READ THIS FIRST -- ONE SAMPLE SET: 474
===============================================================================

 data/cohort_474/sample_list.txt is the canonical list. Normalise every other
 file in the project to it.

   480  deposited
    -4  sample mix-ups                 (fail deposited QC flag)
    -2  missing six of nine covariates (fail deposited QC flag)
   474  cohort_474

 NO QUALITY SCREEN IS APPLIED. Krebs et al. state twice that no sample was
 removed for low quality; they adjust instead, with RIN and the three
 sequencing-metric PCs as covariates and voom precision weights for depth. We
 follow them. depth_ok and rin_ok are recorded in the metadata, so the screened
 subset (461 samples) is one line away for a sensitivity analysis.

 The older 431-sample set in cibersortx/ is a strict subset: 444 deposited
 QC-pass minus 13 on quality. It needs rebuilding against sample_list.txt.

 data/raw_as_deposited/                                              [RAW]
   The two source files, exactly as GEO serves them. Never edited, never
   regenerated. Everything else in the project derives from these two.

   GSE124326_count_matrix.txt.gz    16.8 MB  Raw integer HTSeq counts,
                                             57,820 genes x 480 samples.
   GSE124326_series_matrix.txt.gz   79.5 KB  Sample metadata, 35 fields x 480.
   CHECKSUMS.sha256                          SHA-256 of both; verified before
                                             every read.
   README.md                        [DOC]    Download URLs, and the four things
                                             that break a naive read (titles vs
                                             GSM ids, leading digits vs R's
                                             make.names, ragged metadata lines,
                                             the 47 ENSGR duplicate rows).

 data/build_cohort.py                                               [CODE]
   Builds the 474-sample cohort from raw_as_deposited/. Removes 6 samples and
   nothing else -- no gene filter, no normalisation, no quality screen. Two
   exclusion rules: sample mix-ups and six-of-nine missing covariates. Depth
   and RIN are computed and FLAGGED but never applied. Derives every exclusion
   list by rule rather than by hard-coded ids, and fails rather than writing if
   the two raw files disagree on the sample set.
   Run:  python data/build_cohort.py

 data/cohort_474/                                                 [OUTPUT]
   metadata_474_imputed.csv         THE COMPLETE DATASET. 474 rows x 65 cols,
                                    no missing values. Carries tobacco_observed,
                                    tobacco_imp_01..imp_20 (the 20 completed
                                    datasets -- run the analysis on all twenty
                                    and pool), tobacco_posterior_mean (plotting
                                    only, never analyse it as observed) and
                                    tobacco_was_imputed.
   sample_list.txt                  THE CANONICAL LIST. 474 sample titles, one
                                    per line. Normalise every other file to it.
   counts_474.tsv.gz               16.1 MB  57,820 genes x 474 samples, raw
                                            counts, columns in metadata order.
   metadata_474.csv                 The same 474 rows before imputation, plus
                                            qc_pass, n_missing_covariates,
                                            library_size, depth_ok, rin_ok.
   excluded_samples.csv             The 6 dropped samples and why.
   README.md              [DOC]     How to use the 20 imputed columns, and why
                                    no quality screen is applied.

 data/impute_tobacco.py                                             [CODE]
   THE IMPUTATION METHOD WE USE. Fills the 30 missing tobacco values by
   drawing p* ~ Beta(1+smokers, 1+non-smokers) within each diagnosis group,
   then a Bernoulli draw per subject, repeated for m = 20 datasets, pooled by
   Rubin's rules. Reads no gene expression, so there is no circularity.
   Includes a self-contained Student-t implementation (scipy is not installed).
   Run:  python data/impute_tobacco.py
         python data/impute_tobacco.py --delta 0.912     (MNAR sensitivity)

 data/imputed_tobacco/                                            [OUTPUT]
   tobacco_imputations.csv          474 x 20 completed tobacco columns.
   pooled_estimates.csv             Rubin-pooled estimates, standard errors,
                                    95% CIs, degrees of freedom, fraction of
                                    missing information, and the
                                    assumption-free bounds alongside.
   imputed_subjects.csv             The 30 imputed subjects and the share of
                                    the 20 draws that called each a smoker.
   *_delta+0.912.csv                The same three files under the MNAR
                                    sensitivity analysis. Suffixed so a
                                    sensitivity run can never overwrite the
                                    primary result.

   WHERE delta = 0.912 CAME FROM. A now-deleted side experiment imputed tobacco
   from four smoking marker genes (GPR15, LRRN3, SEMA6B, CDKN1C) and found the
   30 missing subjects carry the smoker expression signature -- GPR15 z-score
   +0.46 against -0.42 for observed non-smokers and +0.94 for observed smokers,
   with plate, library size and RIN balanced between the groups. That implies
   the missing subjects smoke at roughly 37% rather than the observed 19%, a
   log-odds shift of +0.912. It is evidence the missingness is NOT at random --
   ordinary under-reporting of a stigmatised behaviour. Treat 0.912 as a
   calibration input estimated from expression, not a measurement, and report
   it as a range rather than a point.

===============================================================================
 Paper reproductions -- reference, not part of our pipeline
===============================================================================

 Krebs Paper/            78 files    [EXT] [CODE] [OUTPUT]
   Full reproduction of Krebs et al. 2020 (Psychological Medicine), the paper
   that deposited this dataset. Self-documenting: see its STATUS.md, README.md
   and REPRODUCIBILITY.md. Holds its own copy of the two raw GEO files, which
   is where our raw_as_deposited/ copies came from (checksums verified
   identical). Also holds the authors' supplementary DE and WGCNA tables.

 Boltz Paper/           108 files    [EXT] [CODE] [OUTPUT]
   Reproduction of Boltz et al. 2024 (AJHG), the paper whose single-sentence
   prediction claim Arm 1 tests properly. Includes their supplementary tables,
   the paper PDF and full text, and the DICE reference expression data.
